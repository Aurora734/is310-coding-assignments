# Space-Time Labyrinth of Classical Architecture
by Aurora Johnston

## Introduction

Hello curious scholar! Come along on an adventure through time and space to explore classical art and architecture! You will explore the maze of spacetime to locate one particular work of art.

I'll give you one hint to start you off on your journey: this work can be found at UIUC's very own Spurlock Museum! Though I won't tell you whether it's the original or a reproduction. Enter your time machine to begin exploring. Good luck!

## Tips for moving through time and space
- Enter directories by using chdir [directory] or cd [directory]
- Exit directories by using sl .. or cd ..
- Open files by using open [file]
- If you're a Windows' PowerShell user, first run the following command in order to reveal hidden files**:
        
        .\hide-dotfiles.ps1

**If this does not work, please reach out so that I may try to fix it.

## Author's Notes
1) I didn't include many clues, and the maze is rather large. If you are having trouble, *please* reach out to me, and I will give you an additional hint.

2) Please give grace to any wibbly-wobbly chronology or possibly incorrect facts. History is not an exact science, and many fun facts are representative of one scholarly or historical interpretation rather than a solidified truth. Additionally, the temporal disparities between different monuments, artworks, and various portions of structures across space make it so that the timelines within this maze are also the opposite of exact. This is really all just in good fun.

3) Yes, my use of the phrases "wibbly-wobbly" and "timey-wimey" are references to Doctor Who.
