EPIC: THE MUSICAL

This is a musical adaptation of Homer's The Odyssey. It is comprised of nine concept albums or "sagas" as it is a concept musical rather than the soundtrack for a stage production. The creator, Jorge Rivera-Herrans, has an official playlist with all of the songs in order on his Spotify page. This is especially important because the sagas were released out of order. See below for the story-based order of the sagas.
