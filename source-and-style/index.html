EPIC: THE MUSICAL

This is a musical adaptation of Homer's The Odyssey. It is comprised of nine concept albums or "sagas" as it is a concept musical rather than the soundtrack for a stage production. The creator, Jorge Rivera-Herrans, has an official playlist with all of the songs in order on his Spotify page. This is especially important because the sagas were released on Spotify out of order. See below for the story-based order of the sagas with the dates of their original debuts.

ACT 1 SAGAS

  1. THE TROY SAGA - debuted 12/25/2022
  2. THE CYCLOPS SAGA - debuted 01/27/2023
  3. THE OCEAN SAGA - debuted 12/25/2023
  4. THE CIRCE SAGA - debuted 02/14/2024
  5. THE UNDERWORLD SAGA - debuted 04/26/2024

ACT 2 SAGAS

  1. THE THUNDER SAGA - 07/04/2024
  2. THE WISDOM SAGA - 08/30/2024
