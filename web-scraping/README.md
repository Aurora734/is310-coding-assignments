### Robots.txt File
https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/robots.txt
This page disallows a lot of bot activity (mainly, specific bots), but not user activity (i.e. human webscrapers). It also has a link to more information on the sites Creative Commons liscense and details on the specifics of that: https://meta.weirdgloop.org/w/Meta:Copyrights. Essentially, you're allowed to use anything on the site so long as you:
1) aren't using it for commercial purposes
2) properly attribute the site as the source of what you are using
3) require the same creative commons rules for your end product

## My Investigation
### How I Chose My Question
I looked around the wiki and found the "Lore" category (https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Lore) of pages. This really intrigued me since I believe this list of pages would contain the most interesting and comprehensive information about the fictional world. I found that there was a subcategory called "Lore by region" (https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Lore_by_region), which seemed like the best way to break down the world into sortable parts. Since the list of lore by region links to lists of pages rather than pages themselves, I decided it would be best to narrow my focus even more to some specific regions.\
Because I'm more interested in and knowledgable about the show Arcane, which is set in the fictional twin cities of Piltover (https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Lore_of_Piltover) and Zaun (https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Category:Lore_of_Zaun) (really that's an oversimplification of the regions, but I digress), than League of Legends as a whole, which has a much larger map to explore, I decided to just compare the list of lore pages for the twin cities.\
Upon exploring the specific pages, I was very intrigued by the different formats of the items of focus (music videos, short stories, comics, etc.), so I decided that I would investigate how many of each type of artifact made up the lore of each city.

### Why This Would be of Interest
For people analyzing Arcane, comparing and contrasting Zaun and Piltover is incredibly important. The show is centered around the unequal power dynamic between the two cities, with Piltover being downright oppressive to Zaun (referred to by Piltover as "the Undercity"). The differences between how the two cities have been represented by officially released media is especially interesting in the context of medium/mode of representation, which is what I'm investigating. The creators' of the show were incredibly intentional about the artistic differences and similarities in how the two cities were represented (color scheme, medium, editing style, character design, environment design, prop design, etc.), so it'd be incredibly interesting to see if there are any notable artistic differences in the lore of the two cities. Does Zaun have more comics because it's the more artistically expressive city? Does Piltover have more short stories because it's the more academic city? I'll let you know once I've completed my investigation!
